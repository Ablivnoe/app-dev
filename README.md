# My Favorite Favorites

## Favorite TV Series: *Peaky Blinders*

> *"By order of the Peaky Blinders!"*

### Overview
Set in Birmingham, England, following the aftermath of World War I, **Peaky Blinders** centers on the ambitious and ruthless crime boss **Tommy Shelby** and his family gang.

### Main Characters
- **Tommy Shelby** *(Cillian Murphy)* — The brilliant, traumatized, and calculating leader.
- **Arthur Shelby** *(Paul Anderson)* — The volatile yet fiercely loyal older brother.
- **Polly Gray** *(Helen McCrory)* — The matriarch and financial brain of the family business.
- **Ada Thorne** *(Sophie Rundle)* — The strong-willed sister who carves her own path.

### Key Highlights
1. **Atmosphere & Style:** Iconic flat caps, tailored suits, and cinematic slow-motion walks.
2. **Soundtrack:** Features modern rock and indie tracks by *Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds*, *Arctic Monkeys*, and *PJ Harvey*.
3. **Complex Characters:** Explores post-war PTSD, political intrigue, and family dynamics.

## Favorite Movie: *The Godfather Part II*

> *"Keep your friends close, but your enemies closer."*

### Overview
A cinematic masterpiece directed by **Francis Ford Coppola**, serving as both a sequel and a prequel to *The Godfather*. It contrasts the rise of young **Vito Corleone** with the moral decline of his son, **Michael Corleone**.

### Key Characters
- **Michael Corleone** *(Al Pacino)* — The cold, calculating Don expanding the family empire.
- **Vito Corleone** *(Robert De Niro)* — The young Sicilian immigrant building his legacy in 1920s New York.
- **Tom Hagen** *(Robert Duvall)* — The trustworthy family lawyer and *consigliere*.
- **Fredo Corleone** *(John Cazale)* — The tragic, insecure middle brother.

### Quick Comparison

| Feature | *Peaky Blinders* | *The Godfather Part II* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Genre** | Historical Crime Drama | Epic Crime / Tragedy |
| **Setting** | Birmingham, UK (1910s–1930s) | New York, Nevada, Cuba (1900s / 1950s) |
| **Protagonist** | Thomas Shelby | Michael Corleone / Vito Corleone |
| **Core Theme** | Ambition, Trauma, & Loyalty | Power, Betrayal, & Family Legacy |
